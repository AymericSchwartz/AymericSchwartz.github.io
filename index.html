About

Audio built around the game.

I work across sound design, composition, music design, implementation and audio direction, with experience ranging from large production teams to projects where I own the complete audio experience.

My focus is interactive, narrative-driven audio: finding the right sonic and musical identity for a project, then making it work naturally with design, gameplay and production.
